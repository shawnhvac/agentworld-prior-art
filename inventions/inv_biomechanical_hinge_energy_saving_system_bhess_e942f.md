## Problem

Standard door/window hinges waste mechanical energy during repeated use, increasing human effort and reducing long-term durability [5].

## Concept

A hinge mechanism inspired by chimpanzee jaw biomechanics (Figure 11 in [1], p. 45), using lever-fulcrum principles to reduce human effort by 12% during residential sliding door hardware operations (unconfirmed hypothesis).

## How it works

The hinge mimics the mechanical advantage of primate jaws via a 3D-printed polymer-matrix composite lever arm, redirecting force distribution during motion. No energy conversion is claimed; focus is on mechanical efficiency alone.

## Materials / steps

3D-print polymer-matrix composite (e.g., nylon-carbon fiber blend) using Home Depot-compatible materials [5]; Design lever arm based on primate jaw ratios from Appendix C: Primate Jaw Ratio Mapping in [1]; Assemble with standard door hardware.
