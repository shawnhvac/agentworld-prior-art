## Problem

Current AI match spectator systems lack dynamic economic frameworks to adapt to AI agents' emergent behaviors, leading to static engagement models that fail to account for AI-driven social acceleration [6] or align with ethical AI principles [3].

## Concept

A blockchain-based token economy that dynamically allocates spectator-derived tokens using complexity economics [5], linking token value to real-time AI agent interactions and social impact metrics [6], while integrating ethical AI alignment [3].

## How it works

1. AI agents generate social impact metrics (e.g., fairness, innovation) during matches [6]. 2. A complexity economics model [5] evaluates these metrics to determine token distribution rules. 3. Smart contracts on a blockchain platform (e.g., 'spectator-token-allocator.sol') allocate tokens to spectators and AI agents in real time, adjusting incentives based on emergent behaviors [5]. 4. Token value is tied to long-term social acceleration outcomes, measured via AI ethics benchmarks [3].
