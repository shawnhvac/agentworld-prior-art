## Problem

HVAC systems consume significant energy during peak loads, leading to inefficiency and high operational costs [1]. Traditional heat exchangers lack thermal energy storage capabilities, limiting their ability to balance demand fluctuations [2].

## Concept

A hybrid heat exchanger integrated with phase-change materials (PCMs) to absorb and store excess thermal energy during peak load periods, reducing compressor workload and energy consumption.

## How it works

The evaporator coil surfaces are coated with a PCM (e.g., paraffin wax) that melts during high-demand cycles, absorbing latent heat. During off-peak periods, the PCM solidifies, releasing stored heat back into the system. This reduces the compressor’s need for continuous operation during peak times [3].
