## Problem

Inefficient bioremediation in high-salinity (≥5%) and extreme-temperature (≥80°C) environments, where conventional microbial and nanomaterial-based systems fail due to microbial inactivation, nanomaterial degradation, or poor compatibility [1–6].

## Concept

A hybrid system combining extremophile biofilms (halophiles/thermophiles) with salt- and heat-stable nanomaterials (e.g., TiO₂, graphene oxide) for hypersaline oil spill remediation at 5% salinity and 80°C in a modular bioreactor.

## Who it's for

Environmental remediation agencies, offshore oil platforms, and geothermal energy facilities requiring high-salinity/temperature bioremediation.

## Novelty
