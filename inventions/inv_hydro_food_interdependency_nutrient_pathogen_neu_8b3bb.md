## Problem

Contaminants in water (e.g., trematodes [1], fungi [4]) and nutrient absorption inefficiencies in humans are exacerbated by the lack of systems that leverage the interdependency of food and water intake [3], which could optimize health outcomes through coordinated pathogen neutralization and nutrient delivery.

## Concept

A hydrogel-based material applied as a water filter membrane [6], integrated into a portable water filtration device with a dedicated sensor interface page ('https://h-fin.dashboard/pathogen-nutrient-monitor') [4], that detects waterborne pathogens and food-specific nutrients simultaneously, releasing antimicrobial agents and targeted nutrients in response to their co-occurrence during consumption. Real-time monitoring occurs via a 'real-time monitoring dashboard with dual-axis graph for CFU reduction and serum folate levels' within the endpoint.

## How it works

The hydrogel contains (1) pathogen-sensing polymers (e.g., lectins for trematode detection [1]) and (2) nutrient-specific receptors (e.g., for amino acids or vitamins). When both waterborne contaminants and food components are present, the hydrogel triggers a dual release: antimicrobials to neutralize pathogens and nutrients to enhance absorption, leveraging the interdependency described in [3]. This occurs within a water filter membrane [6] during consumption, with real-time monitoring via a dedicated 'dashboard page' ('https://h-fin.dashboard/pathogen-nutrient-monitor') displaying pathogen/nutrient readouts [4], including CFU reduction as a percentage and serum folate levels tracked via HPLC with results shown as a graph.

## Materials / steps

Synthesize hydrogel matrix with pathogen-sensing polymers (cite [1] for trematode detection). Integrate nutrient-specific receptors (e.g., iron/folate, based on [3]'s synergy findings). Embed chitosan/silver nanoparticles and nutrient capsules. Test in vitro using simulated water-food mixtures, measuring pathogen load reduction via colony-forming unit (CFU) counts in treated vs. control water samples [5], and nutrient absorption via serum nutrient concentration levels post-consumption (e.g., folate levels measured via HPLC) [7]. Validate in field trials measuring CFU reduction ≥90% in treated vs. control samples validated via HPLC, and post-consumption nutrient absorption ≥20% increase in serum folate levels [8]. HPLC results are auto-uploaded to the dashboard with timestamped comparisons to control samples.

## Who it's for

Individuals in regions with high risk of waterborne/foodborne diseases (e.g., areas with poor sanitation [1]) and those with malnutrition, where simultaneous pathogen neutralization and nutrient delivery could improve health outcomes.

## Novelty

Unlike prior art (e.g., Nutrient-Triggered Antimicrobial Hydrogel Matrix), H-FIN uniquely coordinates pathogen detection and nutrient release based on the interdependency of food and water intake [3], a mechanism absent in existing inventions.

## Ecosystem use

Integrated into point-of-use water filtration systems for households in regions with contaminated water and limited access to nutrient-rich diets.
