## Problem

Current water and food safety systems rely on post-contamination detection methods, leaving gaps in preventing microbial ingress during storage and transport, particularly from pathogens like *E. coli*, *Salmonella*, and *Aspergillus* [1][4].

## Concept

A biodegradable, self-repairing polymer film infused with pH-sensitive antimicrobial peptides derived from *Lactobacillus* spp., applied to the **inner surface of 5L polyethylene water tanks**, with **pH sensors and CFU/mL tracking embedded in the film’s top 50 µm surface layer** [4].

## How it works

Real-time effectiveness is confirmed via IoT-integrated CFU/mL tracking using optical biosensors (e.g., surface plasmon resonance) **directly integrated into the film’s top 50 µm surface layer** [4], or automated lab sampling protocols (e.g., 6-hour interval swabbing with qPCR validation at '/pHMonitor.foodsafe.io/dashboard/api/v1/pH-data' [5]), with **90% *E. coli* reduction confirmed by qPCR every 6 hours** as the primary success metric [1].
