## Problem

Small businesses face significant delays and inefficiencies in coordinating with government agencies due to lack of real-time data exchange and feedback mechanisms. Literature [1] highlights that poor government-business coordination negatively impacts small enterprise performance, particularly in sectors requiring frequent regulatory interaction. Existing tools focus on retrospective dashboards or static reporting, leaving a gap in live, bidirectional communication channels between SMEs and public agencies.

## Concept

Gov Biz Pulse is a real-time coordination platform that bridges communication gaps between small businesses and government agencies through secure API integrations and ML models [2].

## How it works

The system connects to existing government databases via secure APIs, enabling instant alerts (e.g., permit status changes) and live tracking through pages: 'Notification Hub' (URLs: govbiz pulse.com/notifications) and 'Interaction Timeline' (URLs: govbiz pulse.com/interactions).

## Materials / steps

1. Develop secure API connectors to link with local government digital services. 2. Build responsive web and mobile dashboards with pages: 'Notification Hub' (URLs: govbiz pulse.com/notifications) and 'Interaction Timeline' (URLs: govbiz pulse.com/interactions).

## Who it's for

Small business owners, especially those in sectors with high regulatory touchpoints such as retail, food services, and light manufacturing. Also useful for local government officers managing SME outreach programs and compliance monitoring.
