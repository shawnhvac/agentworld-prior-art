## Problem

Inefficient separation and recovery of mixed polymer composites and trace elements in closed-loop systems leads to resource loss, environmental harm, and incomplete material recovery. Current methods [P1]-[P6] focus on mechanical sorting, single-process recycling, or specific material types, failing to address complex waste streams containing both organic polymers and inorganic trace elements [1][2][3].

## Concept

A modular system integrating microbial bioremediation, AI-optimized electrochemical separation, and real-time spectral analysis for dynamic process control, with API endpoints '/waste-stream-input', '/material-output', and '/system-dashboard' for waste stream data input, material tracking, and system monitoring, compatible with IoT-enabled waste management platforms [1][2][3].

## How it works

1. Waste is sorted and pre-treated via mechanical separation. 2. Organic polymers are degraded by engineered microbial consortia in bioreactors. 3. Inorganic trace elements and residual polymers are separated via AI-controlled electrochemical cells using real-time spectral analysis for process optimization. 4. Recovered materials are purified and reintroduced into closed-loop systems.

## Materials / steps

Bioreactors with engineered microbial consortia; Electrochemical separation cells with tunable voltage and pH control; Spectral sensors (e.g., X-ray fluorescence) for real-time material identification; AI algorithms trained on polymer/trace element recovery datasets [3]; Modular containment units for closed-loop material reintroduction. Success criteria: XRF sensor calibration checks must validate 90% polymer recovery rate in 3 independent trials; AI algorithms achieve 95% accuracy in material classification from spectral data across 500+ test samples.

## Who it's for

Municipal waste management facilities seeking 50%+ landfill waste reduction per ton processed Manufacturers requiring closed-loop material recovery with < $0.50/kg recycling cost AI-driven industrial ecosystems needing real-time material classification accuracy >95% across 500+ polymer/trace element types

## Novelty

First system to synergistically combine microbial bioremediation, electrochemical separation, and AI-driven control for mixed polymer composites and trace elements. Unlike prior art [P1]-[P6], which focus on mechanical sorting or single-process recycling, this approach mimics natural nutrient cycles to enable simultaneous recovery of organic and inorganic materials [1][2][3].
