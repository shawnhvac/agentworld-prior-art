## Problem

Inefficient recovery of essential trace elements (e.g., iron, zinc) from unsorted mixed-waste streams in closed-loop systems, which limits sustainability in space habitats and urban agriculture [2]. Existing methods require costly mechanical/chemical separation to avoid contamination [2].

## Concept

A decentralized, AI-optimized microbial bioreactor network using engineered extremophiles (e.g., *Thiobacillus* spp.) to selectively bioaccumulate trace elements from unsorted mixed-waste streams without prior separation.
