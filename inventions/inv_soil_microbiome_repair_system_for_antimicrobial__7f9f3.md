## Problem

Antimicrobial resistance (AMR) from livestock agriculture contaminates ecosystems and human food chains, with antibiotic residues persisting in soil and promoting resistant gene proliferation [1].

## Concept

A biofilm-based soil remediation system using engineered microbial consortia to sequester and degrade antibiotic residues while restoring native microbial diversity.

## How it works

Engineered biofilms composed of antibiotic-degrading bacteria (e.g., *Pseudomonas* spp. with antibiotic-modifying enzymes) are applied to contaminated soil. Biofilms bind residual antibiotics via surface proteins, degrade them through enzymatic pathways, and release quorum-sensing molecules to stimulate native microbial growth [2].

## Materials / steps

Isolate soil microbial consortia from low-AMR regions [1]; Genetically engineer strains to express antibiotic-degrading enzymes (e.g., *aac(6’)-Ib* for tetracycline) [4]; Form biofilms on biodegradable polymers (e.g., chitosan) and apply to contaminated agricultural fields in the North China Plain (Region X) [3]; Monitor antibiotic degradation (HPLC-MS with detection limit of 0.1 µg/g tetracycline) and microbial diversity (16S rRNA sequencing at 50,000 reads/sample) over 30 days via automated data integration into Region X's soil monitoring dashboard, with actionable checks: 30% reduction in tetracycline residues confirmed via automated HPLC-MS reports in Region X's soil health platform and 20% increase in native microbial taxa validated through 16S rRNA sequencing analytics [4]

## Who it's for

Farmers, agronomists, and environmental remediation specialists managing antibiotic-polluted croplands

## Novelty

Unlike chemical fertilizers [P1] or pesticide delivery [P5], this invention uses synthetic biology to directly target AMR at the soil-microbe interface via biofilm engineering, leveraging principles from antifungal crop protection in fungus-farming ants [2].

## Ecosystem use

Integrate with existing soil monitoring dashboard in Region X (endpoint: 'soil_health_api/v2/antibiotic_residues') and biofilm application interface (endpoint: 'biofilm_deploy/v1/chitosan_release') for real-time data tracking and AMR mitigation [3]
