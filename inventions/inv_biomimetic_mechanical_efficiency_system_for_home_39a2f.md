## Problem

Home systems (e.g., window mechanisms, HVAC components) often suffer from mechanical inefficiencies that increase energy waste and maintenance costs [5].

## Concept

A mechanical system for home components inspired by the efficiency principles observed in biological systems (e.g., chimp biting mechanics from [1]), optimized for reduced friction and energy loss.

## How it works

The system uses biomimetic design principles to create mechanical components (e.g., hinges, gears) that replicate the force distribution and leverage mechanisms observed in biological systems. These designs reduce wear and energy consumption during operation.

## Materials / steps

3D-printed prototypes of mechanical components (e.g., window hinges) inspired by biological leverage points; Testing under simulated home use conditions with energy monitoring; Quantified comparison of energy consumption (e.g., reduce energy use by 20% in simulated window cycles compared to conventional hinges)

## Who it's for

homeowners, smart home developers, and energy-efficient building designers

## Novelty

While [1] highlights biological efficiency, this invention applies those principles to mechanical home systems without direct replication of chimp anatomy, focusing instead on abstracted efficiency metrics.
