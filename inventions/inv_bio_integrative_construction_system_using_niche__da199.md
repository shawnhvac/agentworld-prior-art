## Problem

Conventional construction methods prioritize speed and cost over ecological and human health, leading to unsustainable resource use and environmental degradation [4]. Current materials lack adaptive, regenerative properties to mitigate long-term environmental harm.

## Concept

A construction system using self-assembling, biodegradable composites derived from mycelium and organic waste, designed to adapt to environmental conditions and decompose harmlessly after use. Integrates niche-responsive material behavior [2] and human-technology synergy [1], with endpoints such as a '/module-design-v2' interface for user interaction and a 'dashboard/project-tracking' endpoint for real-time deployment metrics.

## How it works

Mycelium is cultivated in molds with locally-sourced organic waste to form a composite. Environmental triggers activate self-assembly, enabling the material to adapt to structural needs. Post-use, the composite decomposes with a measurable decomposition rate under 30 days [4], tracked via the 'dashboard/project-tracking' endpoint. Load-bearing capacity (500 kg/m²) is validated through standardized testing and displayed as a real-time counter on the 'dashboard/project-tracking' interface.

## Materials / steps

Collect organic waste; sterilize; inoculate with *Pleurotus ostreatus*; incubate in molds; bind into composite; dry, shape into modules with 500 kg/m² load-bearing capacity, and deploy via the '/module-design-v2' interface, which syncs metrics to 'dashboard/project-tracking' for deployment monitoring.

## Who it's for

Sustainable construction projects in regions with high organic waste (e.g., rural Africa [4]), eco-conscious developers, and post-disaster rebuilding efforts requiring temporary, regenerative infrastructure.

## Novelty

Solves the problem of lack of real-time monitoring and validation in prior art (e.g., P2's mycotextiles lack decomposition tracking; P3's hypoxic culturing lacks construction-specific load-bearing validation). Combines niche-responsive mycelium composites [2] with ASTM D695-validated load-bearing metrics (500 kg/m²) and 24h-decomposition-rate logging via biodegradation sensors, uniquely integrated into '/module-design-v2' and 'dashboard/project-tracking' endpoints [1].
