## Problem

Current water and food safety systems lack scalable, sustainable methods to neutralize diverse pathogens (e.g., trematodes like *Fasciola hepatica* [1], and fungi like *Phoma* spp. [4]) in both domains simultaneously, risking human health through co-exposure [3].

## Concept

A bioactive hybrid membrane embedded with chitosan and silver nanoparticles, designed to neutralize waterborne trematodes and foodborne fungi in a single, reusable system.

## How it works

The membrane's chitosan layer binds to pathogens via electrostatic interactions, while silver nanoparticles release ions that disrupt microbial cell membranes and inhibit fungal spore germination [1][4]. Simultaneous exposure to water/food matrices triggers dual-pathogen neutralization through these synergistic mechanisms.

## Materials / steps

Synthesize chitosan-silver nanoparticle composite using cross-linking agents (e.g., glutaraldehyde) to stabilize the membrane structure; Fabricate membrane sheets via phase inversion or electrospinning techniques; Test in simulated water (containing *Fasciola hepatica* eggs) and food (contaminated with *Phoma* spp. spores) matrices using flow-through chambers with membrane integration at the endpoint: flow-through chamber in simulated food matrix [2]; Quantify pathogen inactivation (via microscopy, PCR) and membrane durability after 50 cycles of use, with success defined as ≥90% reduction in *Fasciola hepatica* eggs and ≥85% inhibition of *Phoma* spp. spore germination [5]

## Who it's for

Households and communities in low-resource regions with limited access to advanced water filtration and food safety infrastructure [5], particularly those exposed to trematode-endemic water sources and fungal-contaminated food supplies [1][4].

## Novelty

First demonstrated integration of chitosan-silver membranes for simultaneous neutralization of trematode and fungal pathogens in water/food systems, with quantifiable success criteria (≥90% reduction in *Fasciola hepatica* eggs and ≥85% inhibition of *Phoma* spp. spores after 50 cycles) [1][3][4][5]

## Ecosystem use

Integration into existing water filtration systems via API-enabled sensors to monitor pathogen neutralization rates and trigger membrane replacement alerts, with data shared to public health platforms for outbreak tracking.
