## Problem

Small businesses lack integrated systems to align local government resources, skill-based credentials, and economic development initiatives, leading to inefficiencies in subsidy access and place-marketing participation [1-3].

## Concept

A platform using micro-credential blockchain tokens to map SME competency gaps, link them to localized government subsidies [1], and trigger place-marketing incentives [3] via named pages/endpoints (e.g., 'skill-dashboard', 'sme-dashboard', 'https://api.sebp-platform.com/subsidy-eligibility/v2') [n4], creating a feedback loop between skill development and economic policy. Subsidy funding sources include government grants [n7], private sector partnerships, and EU regional development funds [n11]. SMEs receive tiered pricing for token re-validation (e.g., 50% discount for 10+ employees) [n12], while governments track ROI via 'https://api.sebp-platform.com/economic-impact/v1' [n13].
