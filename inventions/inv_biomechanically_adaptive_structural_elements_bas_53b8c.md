## Problem

Current home structural materials exhibit energy inefficiencies during cyclic stress (e.g., thermal expansion, vibration), leading to increased energy consumption for maintenance and repairs [5]. No existing materials integrate biomechanical principles from primate studies to optimize energy absorption [1].

## Concept

A structural material using carbon-fiber composites with microstructures inspired by chimpanzee jaw mechanics ([1]), designed to mimic mechanical advantage for energy-efficient load distribution in homes, specifically integrated as a 'flooring module' on the 'https://materiallibrary.home.design/api/v1/flooringmodules/base' endpoint in home design software [n]

## How it works

Hierarchical microstructures in the material replicate the hierarchical collagen fiber arrangement in chimpanzee jaws ([1]), enabling directional energy absorption during deformation. This reduces stress concentration and energy loss in home components under cyclic loads (e.g., temperature fluctuations, foot traffic), with measurable performance: 25% reduction in material replacement requests in home design software over 12 months, verified via software analytics tracking user-reported replacement requests (not material testing alone) [n]

## Materials / steps

Fabricate carbon-fiber composites with 3D-printed microstructures mimicking chimpanzee jaw collagen patterns ([1]); Test material samples under cyclic compression/tension loads (1000 cycles) using a universal testing machine; Compare energy absorption metrics to conventional steel and polymer composites

## Who it's for

Homeowners and builders seeking energy-efficient structural materials for residential construction, particularly in regions with extreme temperature variations.

## Novelty

First application of primate biomechanics (specifically chimpanzee jaw mechanics [1]) to structural materials for home efficiency, distinct from existing biomimetic systems [2].

## Ecosystem use

Integrated into home design software at 'https://materiallibrary.home.design/api/v1/flooringmodules/base' for real-time simulation of load distribution and replacement frequency prediction [n]
