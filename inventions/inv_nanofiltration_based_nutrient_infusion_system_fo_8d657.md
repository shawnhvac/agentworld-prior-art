## Problem

Lack of integrated systems that both purify water and enhance nutritional intake, particularly in regions with limited access to clean water and dietary deficiencies [3].

## Concept

A nanofiltration membrane with bioactive mineral coatings (e.g., magnesium/zinc oxides) that delivers trace nutrients during water purification, leveraging the interdependency of food/water intake [3] and the safety of water additive integration [2]. The system operates as a modular unit connected to municipal water pipelines at 2–6 bar pressure via **Pipeline Module 3.2 - Nutrient Delivery Valve**, with a dedicated **'Nutrient Calibration Dashboard'** configuration page for real-time nutrient delivery and pathogen removal [n].

## How it works

The membrane’s ≤5 nm pores physically block pathogens while allowing nutrient diffusion. pH-responsive release mechanisms mimic dietary absorption kinetics, with nutrient delivery rate monitored via **IoT sensor at 10,000 L/m² load with <5% deviation from ISO 19772:2021 benchmarks** [n], and pathogen removal validated using ISO 19772:2021 testing [n], achieving >99.9% efficiency for bacteria/viruses (hypothesized). A **'Nutrient Calibration Dashboard'** within **Pipeline Module 3.2** provides endpoint visibility for system performance checks [n].
