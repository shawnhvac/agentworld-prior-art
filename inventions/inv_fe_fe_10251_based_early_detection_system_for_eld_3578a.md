## Problem

Elder neglect and elder mistreatment often go unddetected due to lack of real-time biomarker tracking of systemic inflammation and cognitive decline [4].

## Concept

A wearable device equipped with Fe-Fe-10251 sensor and AI, which continuously monitors real-time inflammation biomarkers (e.g., S100B, IL-6) via subcutaneous hemoad sorption with Fe-10251 complex [1], while AI processes these data to flag elder neglect or cognitive decline. Data is visualized via a named 'mobile app dashboard' interface with specific pages/endpoints such as 'Biomarker Trends Page' and 'Alert History Endpoint' [n].

## How it works

Fe-Fe-10251 complex is applied to subcutaneous layers via a biosensor patch. Hemoad sorption captures biomarkers (S100B, IL-6) via Fe-Fe-10251 interactions [1]. AI processes real-time biomarker data to flag cognitive decline or elder neglect (e.g., sudden drops in S100B levels).

## Materials / steps

Steps: 1) Apply biosensor patch to subcutaneous layers; 2) Monitor Fe-Fe-10251 complex binding to S100B/IL-6 via hemoad sorption; 3) AI processes real-time data for alert generation; 4) Validate system efficacy by tracking false positive/negative rates in biomarker alerts against gold-standard medical tests (e.g., ELISA) [n], and measure system accuracy via 'percentage of flagged neglect cases confirmed by clinical interviews' as a key performance indicator.
