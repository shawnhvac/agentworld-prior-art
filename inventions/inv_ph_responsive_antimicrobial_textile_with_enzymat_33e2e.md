## Problem

Textile chemicals cause cytotoxic effects on human health [3], and existing smart textiles lack integration of biodegradability with health-promoting properties [4].

## Concept

A biodegradable textile embedded with pH-sensitive microcapsules containing lysozyme and hyaluronic acid, which release upon contact with acidic sweat (pH <5.5) to neutralize bacteria and stimulate skin repair in the underarm region of a T-shirt available at [www.biowear.com] [3].

## How it works

1. PLGA matrix forms the textile base. 2. ZnO nanoparticles provide broad-spectrum antimicrobial activity. 3. Microcapsules containing lysozyme/hyalur

## Materials / steps

Synthesize PLGA matrix from lactic acid and glycolic acid monomers [5]; Embed ZnO nanoparticles via sol-gel process for antimicrobial properties [3]; Encapsulate lysozyme and hyaluronic acid in PLGA microcapsules using emulsion techniques [4]; Integrate microcapsules into textile fibers through melt spinning or coating methods [6]; Test performance on the underarm region of a T-shirt under pH 5.0 conditions for 24h, targeting a 90% reduction in bacterial count [7].

## Who it's for

Athletes, individuals with hyperhidrosis, and medical professionals requiring antimicrobial and regenerative textile solutions [9].

## Novelty

Combines biodegradability (PLGA), antimicrobial action (ZnO), and pH-triggered skin-regeneration (lysozyme/hyaluronic acid) — no prior art matches this tripartite functionality [4].

## Ecosystem use

Targeted for athletic apparel, medical garments, and hyperhidrosis management, with measurable endpoints for bacterial reduction and enzyme release in the underarm region [8].
