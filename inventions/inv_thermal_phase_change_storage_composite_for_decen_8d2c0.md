## Problem

Intermittency of renewable energy sources and lack of efficient, low-cost thermal storage solutions for off-grid communities, particularly in regions with unreliable electricity infrastructure [1].

## Concept

A low-cost, high-efficiency thermal energy storage material using paraffin wax and graphene-enhanced phase-change composites to stabilize energy supply from solar/wind sources in decentralized systems, integrating into solar panel thermal management module v2.1 at endpoint 'solar-panel-thermal-module-v2.1/phase-change-integration' [3].

## How it works

The composite material absorbs excess heat during energy generation (e.g., solar panels) by melting paraffin wax, storing thermal energy. During low-generation periods, the wax solidifies, releasing stored heat for electricity generation or direct use. Graphene enhances thermal conductivity and prevents material degradation [1]. System-level checks (30% faster thermal response, <5% efficiency degradation after 1,000+ cycles) are validated via IoT sensors embedded at the 'solar-panel-thermal-module-v2.1/phase-change-integration' endpoint, with data collected through 1-minute interval logging and thermal cycle tracking protocols [4].

## Materials / steps

Paraffin wax (phase-change material); Graphene oxide (thermal conductor); Polymer binder (for structural integrity); Mold for composite shaping; Thermal chamber for testing (lab-scale) with quantifiable checks: measure thermal conductivity improvement by 30% over baseline paraffin wax via lab-scale testing using the transient plane source method [4]. Lab-scale metrics (e.g., 30% thermal conductivity) are mapped to field performance via correlation with endpoint sensor data, ensuring lab-to-field validation through 12-month field trials with 100+ units [4].

## Who it's for

Off-grid communities in developing regions, rural areas with limited grid access, and microgrid operators seeking cost-effective energy storage solutions [1].

## Novelty

Combines phase-change materials with graphene for improved thermal conductivity and cycle stability, addressing gaps in existing storage solutions like lithium-ion batteries and conventional thermal storage [2].
