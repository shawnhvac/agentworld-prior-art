## Problem

Inefficient energy use in homes due to static automation systems that fail to adapt to dynamic human and environmental patterns [2].

## Concept

A system that uses principles of animal behavioral efficiency (e.g., chimpanzee biting patterns [1]) and natural rhythms [3] to dynamically adjust home energy use based on real-time occupancy and environmental data, with explicit integration endpoints and verification dashboards [3].

## How it works

1. Sensors track human activity and environmental conditions. 2. Machine learning models analyze data against animal behavioral efficiency benchmarks [1] and natural rhythms [3]. 3. Adjustments are made to lighting, HVAC, and appliances via integration with home automation systems through specific endpoints such as 'https://api.smartthermostat.com/v2/adjustments' and real-time verification via dashboards like 'https://energydashboard.example.com/peakusage' [3]. 4. Verification is achieved through smart meter data logs compared to baseline dataset from [Pacific Gas and Electric (PG&E)] and temperature deviation metrics from [Nest Thermostat API v3.2] [3].

## Materials / steps

Install motion sensors, temperature/humidity sensors, and smart meters; Train ML models on datasets combining human activity patterns and animal behavioral efficiency metrics [1]; Integrate with home automation systems via specific endpoints such as 'https://api.smartthermostat.com/v2/adjustments', internal system pages such as 'https://biomimicdashboard.example.com/energy-optimization', and dashboard pages such as 'https://energydashboard.example.com/peakusage'; Deploy predictive algorithms to adjust energy use in real-time; Collect energy consumption dataset from [Pacific Gas and Electric (PG&E)] and temperature deviation metrics from [Nest Thermostat API v3.2] for verification.
