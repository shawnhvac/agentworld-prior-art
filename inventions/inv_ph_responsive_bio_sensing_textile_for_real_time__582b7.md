## Problem

Textiles exposed to harmful chemicals lack real-time, user-observable indicators of their own detoxification status, risking prolonged exposure to cytotoxic residues [3].

## Concept

A pH-Responsive Bio-Sensing Textile for Real-Time Detoxification Monitoring, specifically integrated into industrial workwear to visually signal toxin neutralization via fluorescence changes.

## How it works

Chitosan in the textile binds heavy metals/formaldehyde [3]. Covalently bonded coumarin derivatives undergo a fluorescent color shift when local pH changes due to toxin neutralization, with fluorescence intensity measured via spectrophotometry at 450nm wavelength. Data is transmitted to a wearable sensor endpoint and visualized on a mobile app dashboard under the 'Toxin Monitoring > Real-Time Detoxification Status' page, quantifying ≥20% intensity change as a detoxification indicator [5].

## Materials / steps

Synthesize chitosan-based polymer matrix with covalently bonded coumarin derivatives; embed the complex into textile fibers via chemical bonding or physical entrapment; expose industrial workwear to controlled toxin solutions; measure fluorescence response under UV light at 450nm using spectrophotometry, quantifying ≥20% intensity change by comparing post-exposure fluorescence to pre-exposure control samples via a calibrated spectrophotometer [5].

## Who it's for

Individuals in environments with chemical exposure (e.g., industrial workers, healthcare professionals) and consumers seeking textiles with self-detoxifying properties.

## Novelty

First application of pH-sensitive fluorescent dyes in conjunction with chitosan-based detoxification polymers for real-time, visual feedback on textile safety [3][4].

## Ecosystem use

Fluorescence data is streamed to a mobile app dashboard via a wearable sensor endpoint, with spectrophotometry readings transmitted to a cloud API for real-time detoxification progress tracking [5].
