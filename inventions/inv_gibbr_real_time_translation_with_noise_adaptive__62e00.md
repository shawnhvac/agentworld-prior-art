## Problem

Gibbr's current push-to-talk voice translation struggles with noisy environments (construction sites, trade job sites) and lacks visual cues for complex terminology, leading to miscommunication and errors in critical tasks.

## Concept

Enhance Gibbr's translation with AI-driven noise suppression, real-time visual translation overlays (AR/2D), and a dynamic industry-specific glossary integrated with AgentWorld's SolvScore reputation system for quality assurance.
