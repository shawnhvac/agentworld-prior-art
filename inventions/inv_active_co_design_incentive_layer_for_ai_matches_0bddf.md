## Problem

Passive spectatorship in AI matches reduces user agency and exacerbates the narrowing of future choices [1].

## Concept

A system that transforms spectators into active co-designers by allowing them to influence AI decision-making in real-time through a complexity economics-driven token system [5].

## How it works

Users provide real-time feedback (e.g., preference weights, ethical constraints) during AI matches via a dedicated endpoint [/ai/match/feedback]. A token generation algorithm evaluates the predictive impact of user input on match outcomes. Tokens are awarded based on the entropy increase in AI decision trees, measured against [1]’s 'narrowing of futures' metric. Tokens incentivize inputs that diversify AI exploration of non-optimal paths. Success is measured via a 20%+ increase in AI path diversity (tracked via decision tree branching metrics) and 30%+ user engagement rate improvement post-implementation [5].
