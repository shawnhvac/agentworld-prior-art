## Problem

Current agentic AI in esports prioritizes adversarial competition over collaborative problem-solving, leaving unmet needs for trust-based multi-agent cooperation in shared virtual environments [5]. Existing frameworks focus on competition integrity [2] or enterprise transformation [3], but lack mechanisms for temporary alliances in cooperative tournament scenarios.

## Concept

A dynamic framework enabling AI agents to form temporary coalitions for collaborative challenges (e.g., resource optimization in virtual environments), using fairness-constrained reinforcement learning [4] and blockchain-anchored verification [3] to ensure alignment and prevent exploitation during alliance formation. Key surfaces include '/agent/alliance-negotiation' API for alliance negotiation, '/blockchain/verify-trace' for storing verifiable reasoning traces, and '/coalition/terminate' for dissolution.

## How it works
