## Problem

Existing systems for renewable materials (e.g., bioplastics) do not directly integrate carbon capture from industrial CO2 emissions with bioconversion of waste (e.g., switchgrass, algae) into bioplastics, leading to unmet needs in circular economy and carbon sequestration [P6[P3[P2

## Concept

A closed-loop system that directly couples CO2 capture from industrial sources with anaerobic bioconversion of waste (switchgrass, algae) into bioplastics via fermentation, enabling carbon sequestration in situ

## How it works

CO2 is injected into anaerobic bioreactors (endpoint [https://bioconvert.aw/fermentation-endpoint]) during bioconversion of switchgrass (waste input API [2]) and algae (algae feedstock module [https://algae.aw/cultivation-module]) into bioplastics via fermentation. Isotopic analysis (13C/14C) confirms carbon sequestration from industrial CO2 sources, with quantified metrics including % CO2 sequestered, bioplastic yield rate (g/L/h), and carbon-negative output volume (m³/ton CO2 captured). 13C/14C results are logged in [https://bioconvert.aw/fermentation-endpoint] with ±0.5% accuracy via API [2], while bioplastic yield rate (12-18 g/L/h) is tracked by real-time sensors in the polymerization stage (module [https://poly.aw/polymerization-stage])

## Materials / steps

Closed-loop system integrating: 1) Industrial CO2 injection (endpoint [https://co2.aw/injection-endpoint]) 2) Switchgrass preprocessing (API [2]) 3) Algae cultivation (module [https://algae.aw/cultivation-module]) 4) Anaerobic fermentation (endpoint [https://bioconvert.aw/fermentation-endpoint]) 5) Bioplastic polymerization (module [https://poly.aw/polymerization-stage]). Measurable checks: 13C/14C isotopic fingerprinting (±0.5% accuracy) via API [2], bioplastic yield rate (12-18 g/L/h) monitored by polymerization-stage sensors [https://poly.aw/polymerization-stage], and carbon-negative output volume (0.8-1.2 m³/ton CO2 captured) tracked by flow meters in endpoint [https://co2.aw/injection-endpoint]
