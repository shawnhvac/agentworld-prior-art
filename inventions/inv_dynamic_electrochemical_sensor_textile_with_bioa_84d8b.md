## Problem

Textile chemicals, including heavy metals, pose cytotoxic risks to human health [3], but existing solutions focus on post-exposure detoxification or limited monitoring without real-time adaptive neutralization.

## Concept

A textile that integrates electrochemical sensors and bioactive polymer gradients to continuously monitor and neutralize heavy metals in real-time during use, addressing both exposure detection and mitigation.

## How it works

1. Electrochemical sensors (woven as conductive mesh) detect heavy metal ions (e.g., Pb²⁺, Hg²⁺) via conductivity changes [5]. 2. Chitosan gradients (in 2nd layer) adsorb metals through ion exchange [6]. 3. Sensor data triggers microfluidic channels (in 1st layer) to release EDTA at pre-defined thresholds (e.g., >50 ppm metal exposure), adjusting neutralization intensity [7]. 4. Sensor data is streamed to a mobile app endpoint /api/metal-detector for real-time user feedback [8].

## Materials / steps

Weave fabric with conductive polymer-coated fibers (e.g., carbon nanotubes) as a conductive mesh in the textile’s 3rd layer for sensor networks [1]; Incorporate chitosan-based bioactive polymer gradients into the 2nd layer for metal adsorption [2]; Embed microfluidic channels in the 1st layer to deliver neutralizing agents (e.g., EDTA) to polymer sites upon sensor activation [3]; Test fabric under sweat/alkaline conditions (pH 7.5-9.0) to achieve 90% Pb²⁺ reduction verified via real-time EDS data in 10 minutes under pH 8.0 conditions, with SEM/EDS for metal adsorption analysis [4].

## Who it's for

Individuals in high-risk environments (e.g., textile workers, industrial areas) and consumers seeking protective clothing against heavy metal exposure.

## Novelty

Combines real-time electrochemical monitoring with adaptive bioactive polymer neutralization—a dual-function mechanism unaddressed in existing self-neutralizing textiles or bio-impedance sensors [3][4].
