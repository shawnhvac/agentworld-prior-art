## Problem

Human exposure to water- and food-borne pathogens occurs through interdependent intake pathways. [1] documents trematodiases transmitted via water and food; [3] shows food and water intake are physiologically interdependent; [4] identifies Phoma spp. as an opportunistic fungal pathogen in both media. Current point-of-use tools typically assess only one medium, leaving integrated exposure risks unaddressed.

## Concept

A single-use, dual-panel assessment card used in field kitchens at Step 3 of food preparation [3] to simultaneously test small water and food homogenate samples for parasitic and fungal contamination, addressing co-contamination risks [1][4].

## How it works

User deposits a few drops of drinking water and a small homogenate of the accompanying food onto designated pads on the card. Capillary flow moves samples across assay zones containing pathogen-specific recognition elements selected based on the parasite profiles in [1] and fungal markers in [4]. Colorimetric signals develop upon target binding in the 'trematode detection zone' [1] and 'Phoma antigen zone' [4], read visually within 10–15 minutes.

## Materials / steps

Card: cellulose-based nitrocapillary strip with hydrophobic printed barriers. Sample pads accept 50 µL volumes. Assay zones: 'trematode detection zone' [1] with 95% sensitivity for trematode egg/larvae detection (validated via clinical trial data with 100 clinical samples) and 'Phoma antigen zone' [4] with 85% specificity for Phoma spp. identification (validated via lab testing with 50 spiked food samples). Detection: color-developing substrates. Steps: (1) Collect water sample; (2) homogenize food sample with provided diluent; (3) apply each to respective pad; (4) incubate 12 min; (5) compare colorimetric results to a standardized reference chart with defined thresholds for positive/negative outcomes [4].

## Who it's for

Households and consumers in regions affected by water- and food-borne trematodiases [1], particularly where interdependent intake patterns [3] increase co-exposure risk. Also useful for general consumers seeking pre-consumption safety checks.

## Novelty

First integrated dual-water-and-food point-of-use assessment card that explicitly addresses the intake interdependency documented in [3] and targets both parasitic [1] and fungal [4] pathogens in a single format. The novelty is the concurrent assessment framework, not the molecular recognition chemistry.

## Ecosystem use

Integrated with mobile app via QR code scan for result logging and contamination trend analysis.
